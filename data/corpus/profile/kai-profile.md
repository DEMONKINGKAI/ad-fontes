# Who Kai is

Archit Sharma — goes by **Kai**. AI/ML engineer, MSc Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at TU Darmstadt, based in Germany. Holds a BTech in Computer Science & Engineering with an AI/ML/Robotics specialisation. Co-founded a university tech club and has held leadership roles.

## Positioning (in Kai's own words from the portfolio site)
"Building systems that reason, not just generate." Work spans the full stack of applied ML: NLP pipelines, generative AI on Gemini and Amazon Titan, computer vision with CNNs and ViTs, reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making, and MLOps across GCP, AWS, and Azure — comfortable taking a model from research prototype to production API.

Interests span multiple paradigms: LLMs, RAG pipelines, multimodal models that reason jointly over text and images, document understanding and visual QA; in RL, policy gradient methods, Q-learning, and model-based approaches, particularly where RL intersects with causal reasoning; on the structured side, causal inference, recommender systems, and representation learning.

## Design philosophy
A clean separation of concerns: **deterministic logic owns state and decisions; learned models handle perception and language.** Systems that reason explicitly are easier to audit, extend, and trust, whether the input is a sentence, an image, or an environment observation. This principle is visible across the portfolio — Threadfall (engine decides, LLM narrates), fons iuris (retrieval + verification constrain the LLM), evidentia (Bayesian network reasoning trace instead of a black box).

## Core technical identity
Causal inference and probabilistic graphical models, and their intersection with generative AI. Long-standing interest in neuro-symbolic hybrid architectures.

## What Kai is looking for
Open to full-time roles in AI/ML engineering, causal inference, and applied generative AI. Also seeking working-student (Werkstudent) roles that accommodate writing a master's thesis. German language level: A2, disclosed honestly where German is required.
